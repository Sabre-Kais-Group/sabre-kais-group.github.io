Ethan Dickey is a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Purdue University, advised by Sabre Kais and Alexandros Psomas. His research explores quantum-like behavior in classical networks, including graph-based constructions of quantum-like bits, their dynamics, and the emergence of entanglement-like states.

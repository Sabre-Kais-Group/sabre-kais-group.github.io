Ph.D. Student at North Carolina State University, exploring how quantum computers and AI can help us understand and design new molecules and materials.

- Teaching AI to think like a chemist: I build machine-learning models that find the most important parts of a molecule, so quantum computers can focus their power where it matters most.
  Putting quantum computers to work: I run chemistry calculations on real IBM quantum computers and test them against the best classical methods.
- Discovering new physics in molecules: I study how the shape of a molecule, such as whether it is left- or right-handed, can create surprising quantum behavior.
- Bringing computers together: I connect supercomputers, AI, and quantum processors so that each one handles the part of a problem it does best.
- Looking ahead: I’m excited about AI systems that help design quantum algorithms, speeding up discoveries in medicine, energy, and materials.
